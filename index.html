Michael T. Kiley
Economist — Federal Reserve Board

I am an economist at the Federal Reserve Board. My research focuses on monetary policy, macroeconomic dynamics, financial markets, and economic forecasting.

This website brings that research to current data and conditions, with regularly updated estimates of inflation, economic activity, monetary policy expectations, interest rates, and financial conditions. The analysis draws on models developed in my research and on independent implementations of other established approaches.

My CV and publications are also available here.

Data & Analysis

Monetary Policy & Financial Markets

Monetary Policy Expectations
Market-implied policy rate expectations and the pass-through of FOMC announcement surprises in Treasury yields to equity prices and corporate borrowing costs.
Financial Conditions
Daily replications of the Federal Reserve Board’s FCI-G, which measures financial conditions by their effect on GDP growth, and of a Goldman-style FCI, with the drivers of each.
Yield Curve Tracker
Treasury yields, term premiums, and estimates of longer-run interest rate expectations.

Inflation & Economic Activity

Inflation Forecast
Alternative models of inflation persistence and the role of expectations in forecasting core CPI inflation.
Real GDP Tracker
Daily nowcasts of U.S. real GDP growth using an independent implementation of the Atlanta Fed’s GDPNow methodology.

Longer-Run Conditions & Risks

Equilibrium Real Interest Rate (r*)
Estimates of the equilibrium real interest rate from statistical models, professional surveys, FOMC projections, and TIPS yields.
Resource Utilization and Risk
Alternative estimates of economic slack and models of recession and unemployment risk.
